How it works

1. Text

Title, company profile, description, requirements and benefits are cleaned and turned into TF-IDF features (single words and two-word phrases).

2. Signals

Simple facts about the ad: logo, company profile, screening questions, salary, length of the text.

3. Model

A linear model trained on 17,880 real and fake postings gives a probability. The cut-off was tuned for the best F1 score.

This is a portfolio project. Use the result as a second opinion, not as the final word.
